# What is on the current usgestalt.org (WordPress), captured October 20, 2026

The site's title is "National Association of Gestalt Practitioners: Join us for a new beginning".
The menu has: Home, About, Conference (2026 Denver, 2026 Conference Registration, 2025 Chicago), Contribute, Ideas, Updates, Contact/RSVP, Mailing List.

**Home:** "The second national gathering will be April 24-26, 2026 in Denver, CO." "During the inaugural gathering April 5-6, 2025, the association created a name, set values, a sense of mission, and a Next Steps Group to shepherd the development of the association." "Please note that this website is temporary, pending collaborative decisions."

**About:** "We're just getting started!" "Initial purpose drafted April 5, 2025: to enliven and expand a Gestalt theory and practice in the United States through shared leadership and participation in research, mentoring, and advocacy." "All individuals and organizations in the United States who put Gestalt theory into practice are welcome to be a part of this process!"

**2025 Chicago:** the inaugural gathering, April 5 to 6, 2025, at The Westin O'Hare, Rosemont, Illinois, in person and on Zoom.

**2026 Denver:** Friday, April 24 (evening) to Sunday, April 26, 2026 (morning) at the Gestalt Institute of the Rockies, 4155 East Jewell Ave. Suite #1100, Denver, CO. Friday covered the NAGP mission, the presentation of legal formation, proposed motions and a vote. Saturday covered work groups on the culture of the organization and the principles guiding the legal entity. Sunday closed the work groups.

**Updates (blog):** "On April 6, 2025, those gathered in person and virtually adopted a name for our efforts: The National Association of Gestalt Practitioners. A sense of mission was identified, activities brainstormed, and a Next Steps Group was formed to shepherd the organization's development." Donors to the 2025 gathering are thanked by first name and last initial ($5,270 raised).

**Contribute:** donations were made by Venmo or by check to an individual (Charlie Bowman, Indianapolis Gestalt Institute).

**Contact/RSVP, Ideas, Mailing List:** embedded Google Forms. The mailing list is a Google Group (unitedstatesgestalt@googlegroups.com).

**Contact email:** usgestalt2025@gmail.com

**Note:** the Council's 2026 developer briefing uses the name **"North American Gestalt Professionals"**, while the 2025 gathering adopted **"National Association of Gestalt Practitioners"**. Both are NAGP. The new site follows the briefing. The Council needs to confirm the name.
