Three looks for the NAGP website

The words, pages and menus are the same in all three. Only the colors, fonts and pictures change.

Open each style and look at its Home and About us pages. Then tell us which one you prefer.

  1. Style 1: Garden

    The current look

    Soft sage green and warm clay, with gentle overlapping circles. Friendly, calm and very easy to read.

    • Sage green
    • Clay
    • Pale sage
    • Butter yellow
    • Oat

    Fonts: Source Serif 4 for headings, Atkinson Hyperlegible for text.

    See HomeSee About us

  2. Style 2: Patchwork

    New

    Warm berry and butter, like a hand-made quilt. The home page shows a Log Cabin quilt in the “Barn Raising” pattern: each block is built around a center square, the hearth of the home, and many pieces together make one larger pattern. Warm, crafted and rooted.

    • Berry
    • Mustard
    • Blush
    • Butter
    • Dark thread brown

    Fonts: Young Serif for headings, Libre Franklin for text.

    See HomeSee About us

  3. Style 3: The Circle

    New

    Sky blue and sunshine. The home page shows a circle of chairs, like the start of a group, with one empty chair waiting for you. Open, warm and welcoming.

    • Ink blue
    • Sunshine yellow
    • Sky blue
    • Rose
    • Sage

    Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque for headings, Inclusive Sans for text.

    See HomeSee About us

You can also mix them, for example the colors of one style with the fonts of another. Whichever you choose, the rest of the website will be changed to match.
