How joining works

  1. Choose a membership. Pick the category that fits you.
  2. Fill in your details. Your name, email address, where you live, and, for professionals, your training and specialties.
  3. Pay securely online, or by check. Card payments are handled by Stripe. NAGP never sees your full card number.
  4. Use your Members' Area. Update your profile, find colleagues in the Members Directory, join the community board, and post announcements.
