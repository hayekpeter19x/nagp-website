Community Guidelines

How we hope to be with one another in NAGP's shared spaces, and what happens when things go wrong.

This is a draft for the Council to discuss, change and adopt. When the Covenant of Community is written, these guidelines should be checked against it.

NAGP is a community of people who care about presence, contact and dialogue. We will not always agree. These guidelines describe how we can stay in good contact when we do not.

They apply wherever NAGP members meet: the community board, announcements, the mailing list, and our meetings, online and in person.

How we speak with one another

1. Practice dialogue

Listen to understand before you respond. Let others finish their thought. Ask questions when you are curious, and stay open to being changed by what you hear.

2. Be present

Write as you would speak to a colleague sitting across from you. If a post stirs you up, take a breath before you reply. It is fine to come back to a conversation later.

3. Speak from your own experience

Say “I”. Share what you notice, feel and think, and own it as yours. Avoid speaking for others or telling people what they “really” mean.

4. Respect difference

Members come from many trainings, lineages, professions, cultures and identities. There is more than one way to practice Gestalt. Difference is something to explore, not something to fix.

5. Assume good faith

Written words can land harder than they were meant. If something seems hurtful, ask what the writer meant before you react.

6. Disagree about ideas, not people

Question an idea as strongly as you like. Do not attack, mock or label the person who holds it. Personal attacks, harassment, threats and discrimination of any kind are not allowed.

7. Protect confidentiality

Never share information that could identify a client, supervisee or student, even when you are asking for consultation. Leave out or change names, places and details. Please also keep what members share in members-only spaces within the community, unless the writer agrees.

8. Keep the board free of advertising

The community board is for conversation, not promotion.

9. Respect privacy

Do not copy members' contact details or directory listings for use outside NAGP, and do not add people to mailing lists without their permission.

How moderation works

The Founding Council looks after NAGP's shared spaces. We will try to act with care, fairness and as little force as needed.

  • Telling us about a post. If a post on the community board does not follow these guidelines, please send the Council a message. Only the Council sees who wrote to us.
  • Council review. A Council member reads every message. We may leave the post as it is, contact the people involved, or hide the post.
  • Hiding a post. A hidden post is replaced by a note saying it was hidden by the Council, usually with a short reason. We will let the writer know why.
  • Repeated problems. We start with a private conversation. If problems continue, the Council may give a written warning, then pause the person's ability to post. In serious cases, membership may be suspended.
  • Serious harm. Threats, harassment, or sharing information that identifies a client may lead to a post being removed at once and an account being paused while the Council reviews what happened.
Please confirm these steps, how quickly the Council aims to answer, and whether there is a way to ask for a decision to be reviewed again.

Raising a concern privately

If something troubles you, you are welcome to speak with the Council privately. You do not need to have a solution, only a concern.

If you feel able, you might first speak directly and respectfully with the person involved. Many misunderstandings soften in a real conversation. If that does not feel right, or does not help, please come to us.

Send the Council a private message, or email {{org.email}}. We will treat what you share with care and discretion.

When we get it wrong

We are a young community, and the Council is made of volunteers. We will sometimes make mistakes. If you think a decision was wrong, please tell us. We would rather hear it than not.

Agreeing to these guidelines

When you start a new topic on the community board, you will be asked to confirm that you have read these guidelines and that your post follows them.
