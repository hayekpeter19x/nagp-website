Moving documents over from Notion

This is the place for the bylaws, minutes and incorporation documents that are in Notion now. For each document, choose "Add a document" and either:

  • paste a share link to it (in Notion: "Share", then "Publish" or "Copy link"; in Google Drive: "Share", then "Copy link" and allow "Anyone with the link"), or
  • paste the text itself into the big box, for example the minutes of a meeting.

Members see their documents under "Documents" in the Members' Area. Public ones are also shown to everyone.
