How to add them to the Google Group

  1. Choose "Copy all email addresses" below.
  2. Open Google Groups (groups.google.com) and choose the NAGP group ().
  3. Choose Members, then Add members.
  4. Paste the email addresses into the box and choose Add members. Google skips anyone who is already in the group.
