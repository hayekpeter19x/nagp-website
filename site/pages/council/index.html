How this area works

  • Only Council members can see these pages. Members and visitors never see them.
  • Announcements from members wait for your approval. Nothing a member writes is posted until one of you approves it. Announcements you post yourselves appear straight away.
  • Every change is saved at once, and the site tells you in plain words what happened. Anything that cannot be undone asks you first.
  • Use the menu above to move between Approvals, Members, Events, Documents, Messages, Payments and Site Feedback.
  • Not sure about something? Ask the other Council members before you act. Nothing here is urgent.
