About NAGP

We are a national community of Gestalt therapists, practitioners, and educators building a professional home for Gestalt therapy in the United States.

Our purpose

NAGP is being created to serve as the professional home for Gestalt practitioners across North America, providing community, continuing education, professional standards, and a national voice for the field.

Initial purpose drafted April 5, 2025:

“To enliven and expand a Gestalt theory and practice in the United States through shared leadership and participation in research, mentoring, and advocacy.”

All individuals and organizations in the United States who put Gestalt theory into practice are welcome to be a part of this process.
