Welcome to NAGP: The North American Gestalt Professionals

We are a national community of Gestalt therapists, practitioners, and educators building a professional home for Gestalt therapy in the United States.

NAGP is in its founding phase. A Founding Council is actively working to establish the organization: developing governance, launching membership, and creating opportunities for Gestalt practitioners to connect, learn, and grow together.

Whether you are a seasoned Gestalt therapist or just beginning your training, you belong here.

Meet the Founding Council

NAGP is led by a Founding Council of five Gestalt practitioners who are donating their time and expertise to build this organization.

The Council has been meeting since mid-2026. It held its first open community meeting on September 27, 2026, and is now preparing to launch membership.
