Privacy

What information NAGP keeps about you, why, and the choices you have. Written in plain language.

This is a plain-language draft, not legal advice. Please have it reviewed before the website launches, and confirm how long records are kept and who to contact about privacy.

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run NAGP and stay in touch with you.
  • We never sell your information.
  • There are no advertisements and no tracking on this website.
  • You decide whether you appear in the public directory.
  • You can ask us to change or delete your information at any time.

What we collect

  • When you join the mailing list: your name and email address.
  • When you send us a message: your name, email address and what you wrote.
  • When you become a member: your name, email address, password, city, state or province, and what best describes you (for example practicing professional, or student or trainee). Your password is stored in a scrambled form that no one, including the Council, can read.
  • If you choose to add them to your profile: your credentials, Gestalt training, specialties, a short bio, your website, and a public email address or phone number.
  • When you register for an event or make a payment: your name, email address, what you paid for, the amount and the date.

How we use it

We use your information to run your membership, send you the news you asked for, reply to your messages, organize events, and keep the financial records that any organization must keep.

The directories

The public directory can be seen by everyone. Only practicing professionals can appear in it, and only if they turn on the public listing in their profile. You choose what your listing shows, and you can turn it off at any time.

The Members Directory can only be seen by signed-in members. It shows your name, location and the details you choose to share. You can leave yourself out of it in your profile.

Payments

Card payments are handled by Stripe, a secure card payment company. You type your card details on Stripe's payment page, not on ours. NAGP never sees or stores your full card number. You can read Stripe's privacy policy.

Cookies

We use one cookie, only to keep you signed in. If you never sign in or join, we do not set it.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies or analytics services.

Who can see your information

  • Founding Council members can see member records, messages and payments, so they can run the organization.
  • Other members can see what you choose to show in the Members Directory, and what you post on the community board.
  • Everyone can see public directory listings, and announcements that are marked public.
  • Our service providers handle some information for us: Cloudflare hosts this website and its database, Stripe processes card payments, and Google runs the community mailing list.

Your choices

  • Members can update their profile and directory settings at any time in the Members' Area.
  • You can leave the mailing list at any time.
  • You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you.

To ask, please send us a message or email {{org.email}}. Some payment records may need to be kept for a time for accounting reasons, and we will tell you if that applies.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page.

Please add the date this policy takes effect once it has been reviewed.
