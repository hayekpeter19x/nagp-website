About NAGP

Why does NAGP exist?

NAGP is being created to serve as a professional home for Gestalt practitioners across North America. It aims to provide community, continuing education, professional standards, and a national voice for the field.

It grew out of two national gatherings, in Chicago in 2025 and in Denver in 2026. A Founding Council is now building the organization. Read more about NAGP.

Membership

What do members get?

The Founding Council is still finalizing the benefits. Here is what we expect:

  • A professional home and connection with colleagues who share your commitment to Gestalt practice
  • Member prices on events, salons, presentations and trainings
  • A listing in the Member Directory, and for professionals, an optional listing in the public “Find a Practitioner” directory
  • The members' community board, and the chance to post announcements
  • A voice in shaping the future of NAGP and the profession

See the membership categories.

Who can join? Do I have to be a therapist?

No, you do not have to be a therapist. NAGP welcomes clinicians, coaches, organizational consultants, academics and trainers, and students in Gestalt training.

People who are not practicing professionals are welcome too. If you value Gestalt and want to belong, there is a place for you.

How much does membership cost?

The Founding Council has not set the dues yet. We will announce them when membership opens. The membership page shows sample prices so the Council can see how the page will look.

Please confirm the dues for each membership category.

How do I pay?

You can pay online with a credit or debit card. Payments are handled securely by Stripe, a card payment company. NAGP never sees your full card number.

If you prefer, you can pay by check. Details on how to pay by check will be posted here soon.

Please confirm who checks should be made out to and the mailing address to send them to.

Directories

How does “Find a Practitioner” work?

Find a Practitioner is a public directory. Anyone can use it to find a Gestalt professional by specialty: Clinical, Coaching, Organizational Development, or Academic and Training. Each listing can show credentials and Gestalt training.

  • Only Professional Members can be listed.
  • It is your choice. You only appear if you turn on the public listing in My Profile.
  • You can change your listing, or remove it, at any time.

How is the Member Directory different?

The Member Directory is only for members. You need to sign in to see it.

  • It is organized by state and province, so you can find colleagues near you.
  • It includes every kind of member, including people who are not practicing professionals.
  • You can choose to leave yourself out of it in My Profile.

Announcements

How do announcements work?

Anyone can read announcements on the Announcements page. Some are only for members.

  1. Members write an announcement in the Members' Area, using “Post an Announcement”.
  2. The Founding Council reads it and approves it.
  3. Once it is approved, it appears on the Announcements page.

You can see whether your announcement is waiting or approved in the Members' Area. If the Council cannot approve it, they will tell you why.

Using the website

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Please send the Council a message and choose “Sign-in help”. Include the email address you use for NAGP. A Council member will set a new password for you and let you know by email.

How can I get help using the website?

We are glad to help. Please send us a message and choose “The website”. Tell us what you were trying to do and what happened. A Council member will reply by email.

You can also email us at {{org.email}}.

How do I make the text bigger?

At the top of every page there are three A buttons, from small to big (on a phone, press Menu to find them). Press the middle A for large text, or the biggest A for the largest text. The website remembers your choice on this device.

You can also zoom the whole page. Hold Ctrl (or ⌘ Command on a Mac) and press +. On a phone or tablet, spread two fingers apart on the screen. More tips on the Accessibility page.
