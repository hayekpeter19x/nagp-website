Membership

What do members get?

The Founding Council is still finalizing the benefits. Here is what we expect:

  • A professional home and connection with colleagues who share your commitment to Gestalt practice
  • Events, salons, presentations and trainings for members
  • A listing in the Members Directory, and for professionals, an optional listing in the Public Directory
  • The members' Community Board

Who can join? Do I have to be a therapist?

No, you do not have to be a therapist. There is one membership for everyone. When you join, you choose what best describes you: a practicing professional (clinician, coach, organizational consultant or educator), a student or trainee, or someone who values Gestalt.

People who are not practicing professionals are welcome too. If you value Gestalt and want to belong, there is a place for you.

For members

How do I get to the members' pages?

Press the green Members button in the menu at the top of every page. If you are not signed in yet, you will be asked to sign in first.

It opens My Profile. From there you can go to Events, the Members Directory, the Community Board and My Membership.

Who can come to events?

NAGP events and trainings are for members. Sign in, press Members, then Events to see what is coming up and to register.

Directories

How does the Public Directory work?

Anyone can use the Public Directory to find Gestalt professionals by specialty (Clinical, Coaching, Organizational Development, or Academic and Training), by state, or by name. Each listing can show credentials and Gestalt training.

  • Only practicing professionals can be listed.
  • It is your choice. You only appear if you turn on the public listing in My Profile.
  • You can change your listing, or remove it, at any time.

How is the Members Directory different?

The Members Directory is only for members. You need to sign in to see it.

  • It is organized by state and province, so you can find colleagues near you.
  • It includes every kind of member, including people who are not practicing professionals.
  • You can choose to leave yourself out of it in My Profile.

Using the website

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the sign-in page, choose Forgot your password? Type the email address you use for NAGP, and we will email you a link to choose a new password. The link works for one hour.

If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, please check your spam or junk folder.

How can I get help using the website?

We are glad to help. Please send us a message and choose “The website”. Tell us what you were trying to do and what happened. A Council member will reply by email.
