Accessibility

We want this website to be easy for everyone to read and use. Here is how to make it work best for you, and how to ask for help.

Making the text bigger

You can make the whole page bigger, including the text, pictures and buttons.

  • On a Windows computer: hold Ctrl and press + to zoom in, or − to zoom out. Press Ctrl and 0 to go back to normal.
  • On a Mac: hold ⌘ Command and press + or −. Press ⌘ Command and 0 to go back to normal.
  • On a phone or tablet: place two fingers on the screen and spread them apart to zoom in. Pinch them together to zoom out.

Using a keyboard

You can use the whole website without a mouse.

  • Press Tab to move to the next link, button or form field. Press Shift and Tab to go back.
  • Press Enter to follow a link or press a button.
  • Press the Space bar to tick or untick a checkbox.
  • The first time you press Tab on a page, a “Skip to main content” link appears. Press Enter to jump past the menu.
  • A blue outline shows where you are on the page.

How the website is designed

  • Large text and strong contrast between text and background.
  • The Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface, designed by the Braille Institute to be easy to read for people with low vision.
  • Big buttons with clear words, and no menus that only open when you hover.
  • Form labels that sit above each box, and error messages that say exactly what to fix.
  • Headings and labels that work with screen readers.

We are working toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

Ask for help

If something on this website is hard to read or use, please tell us. We will help you with what you need, and fix the problem if we can.

Send us a message and choose “The website”, or email {{org.email}}. If you find forms difficult, just send an email and a Council member will help.
