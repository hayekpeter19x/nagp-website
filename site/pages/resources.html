Resources

Gestalt training institutes, professional associations, journals and classic books. These links lead to other websites that NAGP does not run.

This is a starting list for the Council to review and add to. Every link was checked in October 2026. Please add other institutes, training programs and partner organizations, for example the Indianapolis Gestalt Institute and regional study groups.

Training institutes in the United States

  • Gestalt Institute of Cleveland Cleveland, Ohio · founded 1954

    Training programs and workshops for therapists, coaches, and people working with organizations and communities.

    gestaltcleveland.org
  • New York Institute for Gestalt Therapy New York, New York · founded 1952

    The first Gestalt therapy institute, founded by Fritz and Laura Perls, Paul Goodman and colleagues.

    newyorkgestalt.org
  • Gestalt Associates for Psychotherapy New York, New York

    Post-graduate training in Gestalt therapy and a low-fee clinic.

    gestaltassociates.org
  • Gestalt Center for Psychotherapy and Training New York, New York

    Training programs, workshops and therapy in Manhattan.

    gestaltnyc.org
  • Gestalt International Study Center (GISC) Boston, Massachusetts

    Advanced training for leaders, coaches and therapists. GISC partners with Penn State University Press on the journal Gestalt Review.

    gisc.org
  • Gestalt Center for Organization and Systems Development

    Training in the Gestalt approach to organizations, teams and systems, which grew out of work at the Gestalt Institute of Cleveland.

    gestaltosd.org
  • Pacific Gestalt Institute Los Angeles, California

    Advanced clinical training in contemporary relational Gestalt therapy.

    gestalttherapy.org
  • Gestalt Associates Training Los Angeles (GATLA) Los Angeles, California

    Gestalt psychotherapy training and certification.

    gatla.org
  • Gestalt Therapy Training Center Northwest Portland, Oregon

    Training programs, workshops and continuing education in Gestalt therapy.

    gttcnw.org
  • Gestalt Institute of the Rockies Denver, Colorado

    Host of NAGP's 2026 national gathering.

Professional associations

  • International Association for the Advancement of Gestalt Therapy (IAAGT) Founded in 1989 as AAGT

    An international community of Gestalt practitioners, with conferences and member gatherings.

    iaagt.org
  • European Association for Gestalt Therapy (EAGT) Europe

    Promotes the work of Gestalt therapists and Gestalt practitioners in organizations across Europe.

    eagt.org
  • Gestalt Australia and New Zealand (GANZ) Australia and New Zealand

    The professional association for Gestalt practitioners in Australia and New Zealand.

    ganz.org.au

  • Gestalt Review Penn State University Press, with GISC

    A peer-reviewed journal of Gestalt theory and practice.

    psupress.org
  • British Gestalt Journal United Kingdom · founded 1991

    A long-running journal of Gestalt therapy and practice.

    britishgestaltjournal.com
  • GestaltPress

    Books on Gestalt theory and its uses in therapy, organizations and everyday life.

    gestaltpress.com

Classic books

A few of the books that shaped Gestalt therapy. Many are still in print.

  • Frederick Perls, Ralph Hefferline and Paul Goodman, Gestalt Therapy: Excitement and Growth in the Human Personality (1951). The founding text.
  • Frederick Perls, Gestalt Therapy Verbatim (1969). Talks and transcripts from Perls's workshops.
  • Arnold Beisser, “The Paradoxical Theory of Change” (1970), an essay in Gestalt Therapy Now, edited by Joen Fagan and Irma Lee Shepherd.
  • Erving Polster and Miriam Polster, Gestalt Therapy Integrated: Contours of Theory and Practice (1973).
  • Joseph Zinker, Creative Process in Gestalt Therapy (1977).
  • Edwin Nevis, Organizational Consulting: A Gestalt Approach (1987).
  • Laura Perls, Living at the Boundary (1992). Collected writings.
  • Gary Yontef, Awareness, Dialogue and Process: Essays on Gestalt Therapy (1993).
