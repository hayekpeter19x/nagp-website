How to review the new website

Thank you for looking at the new NAGP website. This preview is a working draft. Nothing here is final, and nothing you do can break it.

Three things to know

  1. Leave comments as you go. On any page, press the Comment on this page button (bottom right, or in the dark banner at the top). Type what you would like changed and press Send. Your comment is saved with the page it was written on.
  2. Try it as a member or as the Council. On the Sign in page, choose Try it as an Associate Member, a Professional Member or a Council member. You do not need a password.
  3. Payments are pretend. You can join, register for an event or donate. No card is charged and no money moves.

Things worth trying

As a visitor

  • Read the home page, About and Founding Council pages
  • Look at Membership, then press Join NAGP and go all the way through to the receipt
  • Register for an event
  • Search Find a Practitioner by specialty
  • Sign up for the mailing list, or send a message on the Contact page
  • Make the text bigger with the Text size buttons at the top

As a member

  • Edit your profile and turn your public listing on or off
  • Browse the member directory by state
  • Post an announcement (it waits for Council approval)
  • Start a conversation on the community board

As a Council member

  • Open the Council Area dashboard
  • Approve or decline announcements that are waiting
  • Add an event or a document, such as bylaws or minutes
  • Record a payment received by check
  • Read everyone's comments on the website

Questions to keep in mind

  • Is anything confusing, or hard to find?
  • Is the wording right for NAGP?
  • Is anything missing that members will need?
  • Look for dashed For the Council boxes. They mark text, photos or prices we still need from you.
Names and examples on this preview

Members, events, posts and documents marked Sample are made up so you can see how things will look. They will all be deleted before the site goes live.
